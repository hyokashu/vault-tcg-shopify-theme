# Qilin Cards — Product Context

## Product Purpose
E-commerce store for Chinese-market exclusive Trading Card Game (TCG) products. Sells Chinese Pokémon (all CN-language releases) and Chinese exclusive One Piece TCG items. Products include sealed booster boxes/displays and professionally graded cards (PSA, BGS, CGC).

## Register
brand — marketing/e-commerce. Design IS the product. Every surface builds confidence that this is the place to find authentic CN TCG exclusives.

## Users
English-speaking card collectors and TCG players, primarily in Australia and internationally. Ages 16–40. They:
- Care deeply about authenticity and product condition
- Are excited by exclusivity — CN releases are hard to find outside of China
- Are discerning buyers, not impulse shoppers
- Know their grades (PSA 10 vs PSA 9 means something to them)
- Distrust generic marketplaces; trust specialists

**Scene sentence**: A collector browsing from their desk at home on a Sunday afternoon, bright natural light, genuinely excited to find an authentic CN Crimson Haze booster box — the kind of thing they've seen nowhere else.

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Qilin Cards
- **Symbol**: The Qilin (麒麟) — Chinese mythological creature that appears only during extraordinary events. Symbolises rarity, good fortune, and authenticity.
- **Tagline**: "Rarity is a virtue."
- **Tone**: Confident, collector-grade, knowledgeable. Feels like a specialist boutique, not a generic marketplace. Approachable energy without being generic.

## Anti-References
- AliExpress (cheap, cluttered, untrustworthy)
- Generic Shopify Dawn themes
- Sites that look like templates
- Anything that makes this feel like a general marketplace rather than a TCG specialist

## Visual References
- Pokémon Center official site (bright, brand-authentic, energetic)
- Supreme drop culture (exclusivity, brand mystique, bold energy — not the aesthetic, the feeling)
- Japanese streetwear retail (specialist boutique, collector respect)

## Strategic Principles
1. Authenticity is the core value proposition — every design decision should reinforce trust
2. The two brands (Pokémon, One Piece) each deserve their own visual identity within the store
3. Scarcity/exclusivity language works — these are hard-to-find products
4. The Qilin mythology should be present but not heavy-handed — one elegant icon, not dragon patterns everywhere
